# The WorldForge Philosophy

No one builds a world alone.

Every explorer begins with someone who opened a door.

Every scientist builds upon discoveries that came before.

Every engineer learns from other engineers.

Every artist is inspired by artists before them.

Every teacher was once a student.

Every parent teaches.

Every mentor encourages.

Every friend supports.

Every community contributes.

Stories, history, science, art, and imagination all help us understand ourselves and the world around us.

WorldForge celebrates those connections and invites every learner to become both an explorer and a builder.

---

# Who Is Your Team?

Your team is everyone who helps you grow.

Your team may include:

* 👨‍👩‍👧 Your family
* 🍎 Your teachers
* 📚 Your librarians
* 🏛️ Museum curators
* 🏞️ Park rangers
* 🤝 Friends and classmates
* 🎨 Artists and creators
* 🔬 Scientists and engineers
* 🌍 Communities around the world

And sometimes…

Your team includes the fictional characters whose stories inspire courage, kindness, curiosity, perseverance, or hope.

WorldForge honors the difference between **history, science, culture, mythology, and fiction**, while recognizing that each can inspire us to learn and create.
