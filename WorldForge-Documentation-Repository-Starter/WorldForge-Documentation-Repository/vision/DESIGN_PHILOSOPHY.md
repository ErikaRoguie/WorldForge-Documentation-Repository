# Design Philosophy

## The World, Not the Menu
Navigation should be spatially and narratively meaningful.

## Persistent Personal Meaning
Achievements, collections, projects, journals, and learning histories become part of the Explorer’s continuing story.

## Connected Learning
Subjects connect through real projects and community needs.

## Respect for Reality
Historical exhibits distinguish sourced fact, interpretation, uncertainty, fiction, and simulation.

## Purposeful Technology
Technology serves human learning and community rather than addiction or empty engagement.

## Accessible Adventure
Learning should feel exploratory and remain usable across abilities, ages, backgrounds, and devices.
