# WorldForge as a Digital Civilization

WorldForge should feel like living in a world, not navigating menus.

An Explorer enters through a persistent Home. Family and chosen community are nearby. The neighborhood is active. A guild may be preparing an expedition. The library remembers completed books. The museum preserves discoveries. The workshop holds creations. The calendar reflects the Explorer’s own life. The Marketplace changes with seasons. The Hall of History preserves the past.

WorldForge is designed around continuity, belonging, memory, and contribution.
