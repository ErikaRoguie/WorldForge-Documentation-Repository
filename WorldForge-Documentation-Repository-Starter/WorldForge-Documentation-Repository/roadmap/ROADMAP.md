# WorldForge Development Roadmap

## Phase 0 — Foundation
Constitution, vocabulary, rights framework, documentation, and front-end concept prototype.

## Phase 1 — Explorer Home
Home hub, Library, Museum, Workshop, Calendar, local profile, collections, accessibility, and Journey Log.

## Phase 2 — Neighborhood
Families, chosen communities, guilds, events, permissions, and moderation.

## Phase 3 — Academy
Connected skills, historical academy, project learning, portfolios, and mentor tools.

## Phase 4 — Hall of History
Sourced exhibits, evidence labels, human stories, and cultural consultation.

## Phase 5 — Simulation Institute
Assumption editor, outcome timelines, uncertainty display, classroom controls, and reflection reports.

## Phase 6 — Persistent Civilization
Institutional partnerships, creator tools, AR/VR environments, and long-term preservation.
