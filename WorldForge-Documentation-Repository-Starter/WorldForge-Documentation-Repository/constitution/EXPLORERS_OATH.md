# The Explorer’s Oath

> I will seek knowledge with humility and curiosity.
>
> I will respect evidence, history, and the experiences of others.
>
> I will honor every skill, every craft, and every honest profession.
>
> I will preserve what is worthy of future generations.
>
> I will create with imagination and responsibility.
>
> I will strengthen my family, my neighborhood, and my community.
>
> I will continue learning throughout my life.
>
> I will leave every place better than I found it.
>
> **Excelsior. We Live.**
