# The WorldForge Constitution

## Version 1.0 — Print in Steel Edition

> Technology will change. Hardware will change. Languages will change. Generations will change.
>
> **These principles shall remain.**

## Preamble

We believe knowledge is humanity’s greatest inheritance. Every generation has a responsibility to preserve what came before and improve what comes after. Every person deserves the opportunity to learn, create, contribute, and belong.

Therefore, we establish **WorldForge** as a living digital civilization dedicated to learning, creativity, stewardship, service, and human flourishing.

> **Excelsior. We Live.**

## Article I — Purpose

WorldForge exists to preserve knowledge and history, strengthen communities, inspire lifelong learning, honor every skill, encourage creativity, support responsible innovation, and help Explorers leave the world better than they found it.

## Article II — The Explorer

Every participant is an **Explorer**. Every Explorer possesses dignity and deserves a Home, a Story, a Community, opportunities to learn and create, safety, privacy, and meaningful control over their experience.

**No Explorer journeys alone.**

## Article III — The Explorer Home

Every Explorer shall have a persistent personal Home containing spaces such as a Library, Museum, Workshop, Studio, Garden, Trophy Hall, Archive, Journal, Calendar, Family Hall, and Collection Vault.

## Article IV — Community

WorldForge recognizes families, chosen families, neighborhoods, guilds, schools, libraries, museums, civic organizations, universities, makerspaces, and research institutions.

## Article V — Knowledge and Skills

WorldForge honors academic disciplines, skilled trades, arts, sciences, service professions, caregiving, craftsmanship, agriculture, technology, business, and civic work.

**Every skill matters. Every honest profession has dignity.**

## Article VI — History

History shall be preserved accurately and respectfully according to the best available evidence. Historical records remain clearly separate from fiction, alternate history, and simulation.

## Article VII — Simulation

Simulation exists to help Explorers learn, test ideas, study systems, and understand consequences. Every simulation shall be visibly labeled and may never replace the historical record.

## Article VIII — Creativity

WorldForge celebrates writers, artists, builders, teachers, engineers, scientists, designers, inventors, musicians, craftspeople, performers, and storytellers, with respect for people, law, safety, attribution, and intellectual property.

## Article IX — Stewardship

Explorers are caretakers of nature, culture, language, wildlife, architecture, literature, scientific knowledge, craftsmanship, and human achievement.

## Article X — Learning

Learning never ends. WorldForge supports lifelong education without reducing learning to grades alone.

## Article XI — Service

Explorers are encouraged to teach, mentor, volunteer, research, invent, repair, restore, protect, and serve.

## Article XII — Design Standard

Every major decision asks whether it helps people learn, strengthens communities, preserves knowledge or history, honors useful skills, encourages creativity, protects Explorer dignity, and leaves the world better.

## Article XIII — Amendment

Print in Steel principles may be amended only through a deliberate, documented process. No amendment may be hidden inside an ordinary software update.

## Closing

> **Knowledge Preserved. Communities Built. Stories Shared. Skills Honored.**
>
> **History Remembered. Creativity Encouraged.**
>
> **Every Explorer Belongs. Every Journey Matters.**
>
> **Excelsior. We Live.**
