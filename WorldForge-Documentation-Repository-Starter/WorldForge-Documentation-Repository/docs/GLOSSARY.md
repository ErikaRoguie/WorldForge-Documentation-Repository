# WorldForge Glossary

**Explorer** — a person participating in WorldForge.

**Explorer Home** — the persistent personal center of an Explorer’s life and work.

**Neighborhood** — a trusted social and civic community.

**Guild** — a purpose-centered group organized around shared skills or missions.

**Hall of History** — the factual historical environment.

**Simulation Institute** — the clearly marked environment for alternate assumptions.

**Print in Steel** — a permanent constitutional principle.

**Journey Log** — a record of learning, projects, expeditions, and reflection.

**AI Guide** — a transparent AI assistant operating within defined roles and limits.
